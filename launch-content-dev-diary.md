## Treść

```
About two years ago I posted about a Slavic overhaul I was building for CK3. You responded warmly — around 500 upvotes, plenty of comments, people asking when it would be done.

The honest answer at the time was: I didn't know.

I was building one enormous mod. Cultures, religions, a custom Struggle mechanic, new dynasty mechanics, expanded events — everything at once. It was ambitious. It was also, I eventually realized, the wrong approach.

---

**Why I changed course**

The mega-mod kept growing. Every time I thought I was close, there was more to add, more to fix, more to balance. I was spending more time managing the scope than actually building.

At some point I stepped back and asked myself: what do I actually want to ship? A polished, documented, finished mod — or an eternal work-in-progress that might never arrive?

I chose the first option. Slavic Enlarged is now a series of focused sub-mods. Each one covers a distinct part of the vision, ships complete, and gets properly documented.

---

**What's in this first release: Cultures & Religions**

Today I'm releasing the cultures and religions sub-mod — the foundation that everything else builds on.

It includes:
- [X] new Slavic culture traditions with genuine mechanical weight
- Expanded Slavic pagan faith: new doctrines, tenets, holy sites, seasonal events
- [X]+ new events rooted in Slavic mythology, tribal politics, and historical flavour
- Full localization in English and Polish
- A dedicated wiki: [LINK]

This is the part I'm most confident in. It's been tested, it's documented, and it does what it says.

---

**What's still coming**

I haven't forgotten the mechanics I showed before. The Slavic Struggle of Perun is in development as a separate sub-mod. [Other planned features] are also on the roadmap.

Breaking the mod into pieces doesn't mean abandoning the vision. It means delivering it in a way that I can actually sustain — and that you can actually play without waiting another two years.

I'll post a dev diary for each major update. This is the first.

---

**One ask**

If you've been waiting since that original post — thank you. If you're new to this project — welcome.

Either way, I'd love to hear what you think after you play it. What works, what doesn't, what you'd like to see in the next sub-mod. The roadmap isn't fixed — your feedback shapes it.
```
