## 4. Channel Strategy

### Reddit — r/CrusaderKings
**Why:** 600k+ members; mod launch posts regularly reach the front page with good screenshots.
**Format:** Launch post with 3–5 high-quality screenshots, a short feature list, and a Steam Workshop link. Follow up with a "dev diary" style post 2 weeks later.
**Effort:** Low-Medium
**Key rule:** Lead with visuals. Text-heavy posts underperform. A compelling screenshot is the most important asset.

### Steam Workshop
**Why:** Primary distribution and discovery channel — players browse by tag and recent uploads.
**Format:** Polished Workshop page with a banner image, formatted description, feature list, and changelog. Tags: Cultures, Events, Historical, Overhaul.
**Effort:** Low (one-time setup; ongoing via updates)
**Key rule:** The Workshop description IS the pitch. Treat it like a product landing page.

### Paradox Interactive Forums
**Why:** Dedicated modding community; forum threads build long-tail discoverability.
**Format:** Mod thread in the CK3 Mods subforum with dev diary-style updates. Link to wiki.
**Effort:** Low-Medium

### CK3 / Paradox Discord Servers
**Why:** Real-time community; mod channels in large servers reach thousands of active players.
**Format:** Short announcement with screenshots and workshop link posted to mod-showcase channels. Engage in replies.
**Effort:** Low
**Servers to target:** Official Paradox Discord, CK3 Modding Coop, large CK3 community servers

### Nexus Mods
**Why:** Secondary distribution; some players prefer Nexus over Steam Workshop.
**Format:** Mirror the mod with the same polished description.
**Effort:** Low (one-time)

### YouTube / Twitch Creator Outreach
**Why:** A single mod spotlight from a mid-size CK3 creator (10k–100k subs) can drive hundreds of subscribers overnight.
**Format:** Short, personal DM to 5–10 CK3 content creators with a brief pitch, feature summary, and offer to answer questions.
**Effort:** Medium
**Realistic targets:** Channels that cover mod spotlights or Slavic/medieval history content

### Mod Wiki (slavic-enlarged wiki)
**Why:** Signals polish and long-term commitment; gives players a reason to return after subscribing.
**Format:** Launch with at least the core cultures, mechanics, and getting-started page populated.
**Effort:** Medium (prerequisite to launch)
